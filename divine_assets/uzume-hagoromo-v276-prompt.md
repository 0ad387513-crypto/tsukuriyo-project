Each cell is a DIFFERENT large, fully visible wind-shaped pose of the SAME long floating silk. Cell top-left: broad horizontal S-curve with both tips lifted. Cell top-right: wind lifts the left end high and folds the center into a large open loop, showing more of the pink reverse side. Cell bottom-left: graceful vertical corkscrew/figure-eight, long tails widening apart, with the fabric twisting so the ivory front and pink reverse alternate visibly. Cell bottom-right: silk unfurls diagonally from lower left to upper right, a broad billowing fold and two distinct free tips, now showing more ivory front again. Keep width/thickness and scale comparable across cells; clear silhouette and visibly different pose in each cell, not four copies merely rotated. Natural fabric folds, supple wind movement, elegant semi-realistic painterly game art, luminous but not oversaturated. Exactly ONE ribbon per cell, centered with transparent spacing, no ribbon crosses cells, no text, no people, no fan, no flowers, no wind lines, no grid borders. True transparent RGBA in all empty areas. Overall atlas exactly square aspect ratio.
