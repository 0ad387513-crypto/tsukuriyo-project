# 創世決戦・画像候補

組み込みの ImageGen で生成した4案。参照画像は `top_mode_icons/genesis4p.png`（4人の外見）と `top_mode_icons/cardlist.webp`（目標の画風）。いずれもスサノオ、ヤマトタケル、アマテラス、ツクヨミの4人だけを臨戦態勢で描くよう指示した。

候補1は元画像に近い4人配置と星の核、候補2は一つの神域での対峙、候補3は星蝕の戦場、候補4はカードリストに近い月夜の色と柔らかな筆致を重視した。候補1・2の生成指示は要旨のみ記録している。候補3・4の指示は以下のとおり。

## 候補3の生成指示

> Create a NEW square key visual for the four-player Genesis Showdown mode of a dark Japanese fantasy card game. The FIRST reference is for the identities and costume colors of exactly four characters; the SECOND reference is the PRIMARY visual style reference: painterly fantasy card illustration, rich deep indigo and violet shadows, luminous yet softly brushed gold highlights, textured pigments, coherent atmospheric depth, hand-painted edges, refined Japanese dark fantasy rather than glossy digital anime or photorealism. Depict exactly FOUR and no other people: Susanoo (fierce red/black male swordsman), Yamato Takeru (silver-haired male in elegant blue-white robes controlling several spectral swords), Amaterasu (radiant regal woman in white and gold wielding a large gold sacred mirror), Tsukuyomi (mysterious woman in dark indigo using lunar power). All FOUR are visibly poised to fight, in distinct action-ready stances, facing inward toward a luminous celestial battlefield at the center. Keep all four faces legible at square menu-card scale; roughly equally prominent, no one tiny or hidden. Make the composition cohesive as ONE scene, with an ancient broken shrine terrace and star-lit night sky; no photo planet or Earth. Different from a rigid four-way quadrant: an asymmetric, dynamic arrangement, but preserve clear character separation and balanced visual weights. Serious dramatic atmosphere. Do not add any text, logos, interface, extra characters, dragons, or malformed limbs. Deliver a clean professional cover illustration, 1:1 aspect.

## 候補4の生成指示

> Square Japanese dark fantasy CARD ILLUSTRATION for a four-player battle. The SECOND reference image (cardlist) is the controlling painting style: velvety midnight blues and violets, subdued gold, softly painted contours, layered translucent pigments, star-dust and swirling nebula, atmospheric color harmony and delicate hand-painted texture. It should look like it belongs in that exact card collection, not like a shiny high-contrast game poster. The FIRST reference image is ONLY for the identities of the four Kami. Draw EXACTLY four recognizable characters and no others, all in decisive ready-to-fight poses: Susanoo, a wild black-haired man in red-and-black with a sword; Yamato Takeru, a silver-haired man in blue-and-white guiding phantom swords; Amaterasu, a youthful regal brown-haired woman in white and gold holding a richly decorated gold sacred mirror as a shield; Tsukuyomi, a dark-haired woman in indigo-purple weaving lunar spell ribbons. Show them as four equally important figures around a single moonlit Japanese shrine battlefield with distant starfield. Each clearly has their own action and silhouette. Their faces and main colors must be legible when reduced to a menu thumbnail. Prioritize artistic style conversion and painterly finish over changing composition. Restrained effects: flowing twilight, scattered starlight, moonlight and soft luminous brushwork. Serious, elegant, fantastical. No Earth globe, no quadrants or grid, no text, no logo, no extra humans, no deformed hands or weapons.
